# Card List

Draft card list for the card game. Cost is the amount of energy needed to put
the card on the board. Attack is how much damage it does; health is how much
damage it takes before it is removed.

| Card | Cost | Attack | Health | Notes |
|------|------|--------|--------|-------|
| Ember Squire | 1 | 1 | 1 | Cheap opener. |
| Tin Sentinel | 3 | 1 | 6 | Walls off a lane. |
| Glass Duelist | 3 | 5 | 1 | Trades up, dies to anything. |
| Marrow Priest | 4 | 2 | 4 | Heals the unit to its left. |
| Iron Chorister | 5 | 3 | 5 | Buffs every other friendly unit. |
| Hollow King | 7 | 5 | 7 | Expensive finisher. |
